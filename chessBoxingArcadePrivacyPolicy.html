Chessboxing Arcade Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy covers the personal data that Chessboxing Arcade ("the game") collects when you send us a bug report or feature request from inside the game, and the optional diagnostics you can attach to a bug report. The game is made by Firebull Studios ("we", "us").

The short version: nothing is sent unless you press Submit on the report screen, and you can preview exactly what will be sent first.

1. What a report contains

When you submit a report (bug report or feature request), we receive:

  • Your Steam name and Steam ID, at the time you send the report.
  • Whether it is a bug report or a feature request, and the text you type. Please don't put anything in the text that you don't want us to have.
  • A report code that the game generates. It is derived from your Steam ID and the time of the report, and we use it to recognise reports that came from the game.
  • Your game version and platform (operating system), for bug reports.

2. Optional match diagnostics

After an online match that had a connection or sync problem, the game keeps a diagnostics file on your computer, and the bug report screen offers to attach it. It is switched on by default when a file is waiting, and you can switch it off to send a plain report. When attached, it contains:

  • Details of the match: game mode, time control, your seat or side, whether you were the host, and a match identifier built from the lobby and start time.
  • Network statistics (for example delay and dropped packets) and technical counters from the game.
  • A log of match events and game warnings and errors, including the moves played, from the moments around the problem.

Before a diagnostics file is saved, the game automatically replaces other players' Steam names and IDs with seat labels such as "seat Red", and removes IP addresses and your computer's user name from file paths. This scrubbing is automatic and may miss something unusual, which is why the report screen has a Preview button that shows the exact text before it is sent. Reports never contain hardware identifiers.

3. What stays on your computer

Diagnostics files are saved automatically in the game's save folder, in a folder called diagnostics. The game keeps only the most recent 10 files (up to 5 MB) and deletes older ones. They are never sent anywhere unless you attach one to a report and press Submit. You can delete them at any time by deleting that folder.

4. Why we use it and our legal basis

We use reports only to find and fix bugs and to consider feature requests, and to confirm that a report came from a real copy of the game. We do not use them for advertising, we do not sell them, and we do not share them with anyone else except the service that stores them (section 5).

We process this data because you choose to send it, which is your consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time by asking us to delete your report (section 6).

5. Where reports are stored

Reports are stored as issues in a private repository on GitHub, a service run by GitHub, Inc. in the United States, so your data is transferred to and stored in the United States. Only Firebull Studios can read the repository. Reports are not published.

The game does not collect or upload anything else about you. This policy does not cover Steam itself; Valve's own privacy policy applies to your Steam account and to the Steam features you use.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep a report while we are still working on it, and delete it within 12 months of closing it.

You can ask us to delete your reports at any time, sooner than that. Email firebullstudios@gmail.com from any address and tell us your Steam name or Steam ID, so we can find your reports. We delete the report and everything attached to it, and reply to confirm.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU and UK GDPR), you may have the right to ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw your consent. Email firebullstudios@gmail.com to use any of these. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change what the game collects, for example by adding an optional automatic upload of diagnostics, we will update this page and the "last updated" date, and the game will ask you before sending anything new.
